Support

Help with Psync for iPhone, Psync for Android and the Psync web app.

Get in touch

Write to support@ajar.dev. Say which app you use (iPhone, Android or the web) and what you were doing, and attach a bug report if you can: in Psync, open Membership, choose Data & privacy, then Bug report. It holds no password, and you can read it before you send it.

Psync is published by Ajar.dev Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14071311). It is an independent companion for Psycle London members, and it is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Psycle.

Things Psycle looks after

Your account, your password, your membership, your credits and your bookings belong to Psycle. Psync cannot create or delete a Psycle account, reset a password, refund a class or change a charge. For any of those, contact Psycle.

Common questions

  • I can't sign in. Psync uses your existing Psycle email and password. If they work on Psycle's own site and not here, write to us. If they don't work there either, reset your password with Psycle.
  • A booking looks wrong. Pull down to refresh My Bookings. What Psycle holds is always the truth: Psync shows a class as booked only once Psycle confirms it. If something still looks wrong, send a bug report.
  • Reminders or the calendar aren't working. Check that notifications and calendar access are allowed for Psync in your phone's settings, then switch the feature off and on again in Psync's Settings.
  • How do I remove my data? Signing out removes your session and your data from the device. Deleting the app removes everything else. Psync keeps nothing anywhere else: there is no Psync account and no Psync server.
