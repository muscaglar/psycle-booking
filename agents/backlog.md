# Backlog — candidate work, none of it requested
Purpose: work that is known to be worth doing, so that you can offer it with a size and a risk instead of rediscovering it. **Propose from this list, do not start from it; the owner decides.** **Read this when** the owner asks "what next?", or you have finished and want to suggest a follow-up. **Skip it when** you have a task: nothing here outranks it.

Size: trivial (minutes) · small (an hour or two) · medium (a day) · large (several days, with a device pass). Risk is what can go wrong for a member or for a release, and the rule that contains it. Where things stand: [HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md).

Every row was checked against [decisions.md](decisions.md): none reopens its one CLOSED row (section 4, device-local times) or a section 6 left-alone item. To PROPOSE from here you need only its section 7 and "Open questions"; read the rest before you change behaviour.
