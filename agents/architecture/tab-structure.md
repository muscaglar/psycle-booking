# Tab structure — what a member sees on Discover, Bookings, Stats and Membership
Read this when you need the whole of a tab in one paragraph before changing part of it. Skip it when your task is below the UI (API, storage, native code): the four rows are long.

## Tab Structure (4 tabs)
| Tab | Name | Contents |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | **Discover** | Live filters (no Search button). Date row always on screen — calendar button first, then Today · Tomorrow · 7 days · Next week · 14 days. Below 1024px every other filter sits behind ONE **Filters bar** (`#controlsToggle`; collapsed at every launch, never stored): while collapsed, the active filters are removable chips in `#controlsSummary` plus Clear; at ≥1024px the panel is always open — a filter column pinned on the left, the day strip and the cards in a readable column on the right — and the bar is hidden. Expanded: Time row — Before 9:00 · 9:00–17:00 · After 17:00 + "Available only" (filters the loaded timetable with no network call; never hides a class you hold or are waitlisted on); studio multi-select chips; class-type pills (+ Strength / Reformer sub-pills); instructor box with ★ Favs and an S/A quick filter (appears once an instructor is ranked S or A) → "Updated … · Refresh" → results (the empty state offers Quick picks and Recent searches pills; error and empty states carry actions and say their reason once): cards with an availability line ("Only 2 left" / "25 spots left" / "Waitlist open" / "Fully booked"; counts only while the timetable is under 30 minutes old). A range of more than one day is shown ONE DAY AT A TIME: a day strip (`#dayStrip`, sticky on phones; each day a small word over its date over its count — seven fit a phone exactly — with one class-colour dot on a day you hold a seat) over a pager (`#dayPager`) — swipe left / right, tap a day, or arrow keys in the strip (no previous / next buttons); a single day keeps the plain heading (Today / Tomorrow) + list. Pull-to-refresh refreshes the window |
| 2 | **Bookings** (panel headed "My Bookings") | "N changes waiting to sync" line while the offline queue holds anything → Rebook hint → "Your usual week" card — a disclosure: folded it is ONE row ("Your usual week" over "N classes · Show" + **Review and book**); open it adds the entries ("2 seats" on one that books more than one) and Update from my bookings · Clear; while nothing is saved it is the "Save my usual week" invitation, which never folds. Review and book opens a REVIEW sheet and books nothing by itself: a dates control (Next 7 days · the next three Monday–Sunday weeks — Psycle books about three weeks ahead — and "Newly opened · Fri 9 – Thu 15 Oct" first when the Monday reminder opened it or the release is under a day old), every class listed with a seat count and the spot(s) it SUGGESTS and why ("Bike 12 · your usual"; Change spot opens the real seat map, which books nothing), a button that counts the spend ("Book 3 classes · 4 seats"), then a sequential run that books exactly the spots shown → upcoming bookings under the plan-usage line ("8 of 12 this month · Resets 12 Oct", one segment per class; two collapsible bars when a class falls in the next billing period). Each card is tinted by CLASS TYPE, carries its own day over the time ("Thu 24" — no day headings), opens the class sheet, and shows: seat chips in the class colour (× per seat when more than one) or "N spaces", "Free cancel until …" (or the one red Late-cancel-window badge), a primary **Cancel booking / Cancel all N** (a calm ink outline — red only inside the late-cancel window) with **Change spot** beside it, and a **More** button (Add spot · Find similar · Map · Share; the same buttons inline at ≥1024px). Only the NEXT class you hold a seat in glows. Waitlist places: badge Waitlisted / Spot offered / Spot available / Waitlist closed, a status line for the phase, and Leave waitlist · Check for a spot · Claim spot ("Check for a spot" leads in the offers phase). Calendar export row, "Show past" toggle, "View full history". With no answer from Psycle yet (offline launch, failed load) a read-only "Saved copy · HH:MM" of the held classes is shown instead of a skeleton or Retry hero |
| 3 | **Stats** | History-sync banner (global) → pinned segmented switcher `#statsSwitcher` (a real tab list) over three sub-pages, one displayed at a time and each built when first opened: **Overview** (the Crisp Colour Stats board: all time as the hero numeral with this month + upcoming, quiet tiles, the week streak with twelve weekly bars, "When you train" — a 7 × 5 weekday × time-of-day grid on the theme's neutral ramp — the class-type bar in the class-type colours, year in review, share) · **Habits** (usual slots, routine — compact class cards tinted by class type) · **Instructors** (instructor map, instructors per month, lapsed favourites, You might like, New to you). Tap a segment, Left / Right / Home / End in the tab list, or swipe the page left / right. A page with nothing on it shows one line. "This month", "All time" and the share image count only classes that have started. Signed out: the Sign-in hero only (no switcher) |
| 4 | **Membership** | Account, plan info, cost tracker, Appearance (theme picker — each chip a miniature of its theme's own chrome — and the **Class colours** control: Off · Soft · Bold, one row per class type that opens its swatches, a live preview card, Reset colours), Settings rows (Bike / spot preferences · Reminders and Calendar sync on iOS only · Data & privacy), Sign out (asks first), instructor rankings & favourites, about/affiliation. (Settings panel = app-focused: reminders, bike prefs, calendar sync, export/import, bug report + diagnostics, Help → "Show the welcome again") |
