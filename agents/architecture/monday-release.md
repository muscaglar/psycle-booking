# Monday release — the 12:00 London release, the iOS Monday reminder, the booking horizon
Read this when you touch freshness around the release, the Monday reminder or its tap, or anything that says how far ahead Psycle books (OBSERVED, advisory — never a block). Skip it otherwise.

**Monday-noon release**: Psycle releases new dates on Mondays at 12:00 Europe/London — NOT "next week": by observation each release opens one Friday → Thursday batch about three weeks out and LISTS one batch further (see Booking horizon, two paragraphs down — the app may suggest and explain with that model, never block on it). `_lastReleaseMs` / `_nextReleaseMs` (`pure:window`, resolved through `pure:gym-time` — never the device zone) make `_windowIsFresh` call any timetable fetched before the latest release stale; a one-shot timer (`_armReleaseTimer`) silently revalidates at the release while the app is in the foreground — re-armed from the clock on every return to the foreground (a DOM timer stops counting while the app is suspended), and its second look a minute on clears a failed first look's 60s back-off (`_revalFailedAt`) before asking. The `nextweek` date preset is the Monday–Sunday after today (`daysAhead` 6 — `_windowEndDate` is start + `daysAhead`, so every multi-day preset counts the days after the first: "7 days" is 6, "14 days" 13); its old "Next week opens Monday 12:00" empty state is gone — next week is long open by the Monday before it (see Booking horizon). The iOS Monday reminder's tap is its own paragraph, next.

**Monday reminder (iOS) — 12:00, pointed at the usual week** (the Android app arms the same eight notifications through the same code, filed under its `new-dates` channel and delivered by an inexact alarm: [android.md](android.md) → "Notifications"): the weekly local notification fires AT the release, Mondays 12:00 Europe/London (it was 11:59 and said "opens at 12:00" until September 2026). native-bridge.js `pure:weekly-reminder`: `_nextMondaysNoonLondon(count, now, wallToUtcMs)` (eight rolling one-shots at absolute London instants through the bridge's `_gymWallToUtcMs`, REMINDER_IDS 9992–9999 unchanged so an older build's are replaced, re-armed 3s after every launch, never prompting), `_weeklyReminderCopy(hasUsualWeek)` (title "New Psycle dates are open"; body "Book your usual week for the dates that just opened." when `psycle_weekly_template` is a non-empty array — `_hasUsualWeek`, the string is untrusted — else "Find your classes for the dates that just opened."), `_weeklyTapTab`, `_weeklyOfferDecision`. The body follows the usual week: the bridge wraps `window.saveWeeklyTemplate` / `window.clearWeeklyTemplate` (every argument and the result forwarded) and listens for `data:owner-changed`, and re-arms only while the reminder is on and only when the SENTENCE would change (`_weeklyArmedWithWeek`); passes are serialized (`_scheduleWeeklySerial`) and read the week when they schedule, so a save and a clear a moment apart arm what is true afterwards. *The tap* (no `eventId`): the bridge lands at once on My Bookings when a usual week is saved, else Discover, then calls `window._onBookingWeekOpened()` (js/app.js) — and ONLY it. That hook decides its route at the tap from the same stored week (`_weekOpenedRouteFor`, `pure:week-opened`): `review` → `bookTemplateWeek({ range: 'newest' })`, the usual-week REVIEW sheet on the dates that release opened — NOTHING is booked by the tap, the sheet books only what is ticked and confirmed in it; `discover` → Discover on the FIRST DAY of those dates, written as the calendar's own pick would (`_showOpenedDay`: `startDate`, `daysAhead` 1, no preset, so the calendar button reads "Fri 2 Oct") but NOT through `onDateInputChange` / `setDateQuick`, the names interactions.js saves. Which dates: `_openedBatch(horizon, lastReleaseMs)` — `window._bookingHorizon(now).openThrough` is the batch's last day where that function exists, else the observed stand-in (the Friday 18 days after the release Monday → the Thursday 24 days after; read off Psycle's public timetable on 2026-09-19, not an API contract). It only says where a tap LANDS — nothing is refused or hidden because of it; with no London zone data the date row is left alone and the list is revalidated. Patience rules, both routes: not before launch is done (`_loadableSearchStarted`); retries every 500ms give up 15s after the tap BY THE CLOCK and as soon as the member has left the tab the tap put them on; only the direct call switches tab. The review also waits for a verified session (`currentUser` — its plan would call a signed-in member signed out) and for NOTHING to be open (`_weekOpenedReviewBlocked`: `_dialogOpen`, `_ownKeysOverlayUp`, the overlay stack, the Booked sheet, any busy Book button; if it cannot tell, it waits) — but it never WAITS for the usual-week sheet itself: with the review already on screen the tap opens no second sheet and arms no retry (a second tap once re-opened the sheet the moment the member pressed "Not now"). Instead the open sheet is ASKED to show the newest dates — by the tap itself only, never by a retry that wakes seconds later (the member may have opened the sheet and started ticking meanwhile) — `window._usualWeekSheetNewest()`, published by `_runUsualWeekSheet` while it is up: a sheet opened before the 12:00 release (to be ready for it), or sitting on another range, had no "Newly opened" segment, and the tap that promised those dates did nothing. It re-plans with that batch first, books nothing, and answers false — the tap is dropped, never queued — during a run, while "Change spot" has the map up, and over a run's results (`st.reported`). The Discover date is NOT saved as the launch default: the hook sets `window._dateRowHeld`, so `saveFilters` keeps the date row ALREADY STORED on the member's next chip tap, and a future custom day does not roll overnight (`_rollForwardMode` → null; once it is in the past it rolls to the week through `_applyDateQuick`, unsaved); a date the member picks themselves (`setDateQuick`, `onDateInputChange`, `clearFilters`, `applySavedSearch`) or sign-out releases it (`_releaseDateRow`). *The offer*: `window._offerWeeklyReminder(reason)` exists in the iOS app only (the bridge defines it; js/tabs.js calls it behind a `typeof` guard at TWO moments — right after "Save my usual week" succeeds, and when the review sheet closes (`bookTemplateWeek`, after the `await` on the sheet only: a usual week saved by an earlier build never meets "Save" again) — so on the web no reminder UI appears where it cannot work). Offered again while an ask still waits its turn, the give-up clock starts over (`_weeklyAskSince`) — still ONE ask: saved, then straight into a review longer than 40s, the offer used to give up a moment before the sheet closed. Once — `psycle_weekly_reminder_asked` — and only to a member with a usual week who has never touched the switch (a stored `'on'` or `'off'` IS an answer) and whom iOS has not already refused: "Remind you on Mondays at 12:00, when new dates open?" (Remind me / Not now; the body says a tap opens a review and nothing is booked until they confirm). Same manners as the first-booking ask: never at launch, only after the UI has been clear for two 1s polls (the blocking ids now include `usualWeekSheet` — for BOTH asks: a run inside that sheet emits `booking:complete` per seat), gives up 40s after it was last called for, by the clock, never into a hidden app, a displaced dialog is not an answer, and iOS's own prompt only follows an in-app yes (`_nativeReminder.enable()`). The Settings row (js/tabs.js `_paintReminderRow`: "Monday booking reminder — Mondays at 12:00 — when Psycle opens new dates") stays the place to change it. tests/suites/14c-weekly-reminder.js holds all of it; the Discover route's patience rules are in tests/suites/discover-journeys.js.

**Booking horizon (OBSERVED, advisory)** (`pure:horizon`, nested in `pure:window`; tests/suites/14a-usual-week-sheet.js): how far ahead Psycle books is NOT in the API (an event carries `bookable_until` only) — it was read off Psycle's public timetable on 2026-09-19: each Monday-12:00 release opens ONE 7-day batch, the Friday 18 days on to the Thursday 24 days on (14 Sept opened Fri 2 → Thu 8 Oct), and the timetable LISTS one batch further than is open (credit types with an `extended_booking_period` may book that one early). The constants are named (`RELEASE_OPENS_FROM_DAYS` 18 · `RELEASE_OPENS_TO_DAYS` 24 · `RELEASE_LISTED_EXTRA_DAYS` 7). `window._bookingHorizon(nowMs)` → `{ openThrough, listedThrough, lastRelease, nextRelease, lastBatch {from,to}, nextBatch {from,to} }` (London dates; `null` without Europe/London data); `_dayOpensMs(day)` / `_dayListedMs(day)` are the release that opens / first lists a day. **It may SUGGEST and EXPLAIN, never BLOCK**: a class that is listed can always be tried, and Psycle's own answer stands. Readers: the usual-week sheet (its three weeks, "Newly opened", the "May not be open yet" note), the Monday reminder's tap (`_openedBatch`, `pure:week-opened`: `openThrough` is the batch's LAST day, so `openThrough` − 6 = `lastBatch.from`), the day pager's `unopened` state and the "Not on the timetable yet" empty state — the last two are about LISTING, so only a date picked in the calendar reaches them. **The model is written down twice** — these constants, and `_openedBatch`'s stand-in pair `OPENED_BATCH_FIRST_DAY` 18 / `OPENED_BATCH_LAST_DAY` 24 for a context without `_bookingHorizon` — so change both together: tests/suites/14c-weekly-reminder.js fails if the tap's batch and the sheet's "Newly opened" batch ever stop being the same seven days. (A suite that fakes `_bookingHorizon` must put its fake in AFTER `t.loadPure('js/app.js', 'window', …)`: `pure:horizon` is nested in that region, so loading it declares the real one over any injected global.) The rule this replaced — `_weekOpensMs`, "a Monday–Sunday week opens at noon on the Monday before it", and the "Next week opens Monday 12:00" empty state built on it — was wrong (next week and the week after are long open) and is gone.
