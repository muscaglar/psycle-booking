Sheets are listed in the order psycle-finder.html links them (later wins a tie). A `crisp:*` section runs to the next `crisp:*` marker; every other range is the banner's OWN lines, up to the next banner of any kind. Indentation shows the KIND of banner (a boxed one, then a `── title` one), not containment: the rules under an indented banner are not necessarily part of the topic, or the `@media` block, of the boxed banner above it — check the braces in the source. `--a-{x,y}` = `--a-x`, `--a-y`; `--a-{x,y}-{p,q}` = all four.
